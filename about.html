About

Lin Yao

I am a photographer working between portrait close-ups and the wide, thinly populated landscapes of the western plateau. The work on this site gathers the last four years into three series: Gaze, Wilderness, Highland Pastoral.

I am interested in the moment just before a pose settles — a held breath, a ridge half-obscured by fog, an animal indifferent to the camera. Most frames are made on a single 35 mm body with prime lenses; the portrait series is printed entirely in black and white.

Every public release is managed as a publish batch: once a set of series is chosen, its cover, photo order and captions are frozen together, so what you see here always corresponds to an approved, numbered version — currently 14 photographs across the three series.
